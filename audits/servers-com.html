AI Compute is a headline product on the homepage and has nothing in the docs

AI Compute is one of the three products at the top of the homepage, and it has its own product page. In the documentation I found no page for it. The product list in the docs sidebar names Enterprise Bare Metal, Scalable Bare Metal, Cloud Servers, Managed Kubernetes and Forex VPS. The docs sitemap has no AI Compute URL, and docs/llms.txt mentions neither AI Compute nor GPUs.

A customer who clicks through from the homepage to learn how to start has nowhere to land, and may open a ticket instead. The same gap affects an AI assistant that answers from llms.txt.

A one-page quick start for AI Compute, or at least a link to its product page at the top of the docs sidebar.

To check: open servers.com/docs/llms.txt and search for "AI Compute" and "GPU".
Lower confidenceSeen twice, not repeated.

A contact form replaced by a cookie message

On two visits in my browser, the "Get in touch" page showed an empty block where the form should be, with a tooltip over the intro text: "Please accept the cookie consent". I had rejected cookies.

I couldn't make it happen again. I cleared the consent cookie and tried again with "Reject All", with the banner's close button and with reloads. The form showed up every time. So I can't tell you what triggers it. I'm mentioning it because a visitor in that state can't write to you, and it may depend on a consent saved by an earlier visit.
